# Project Rolls

All Advantages require rolls or scenes spent working toward them relative to their dot value, aside from Looks merits, which require Fleshcrafting. The rolls or actions needed to gain Advantages are different, so it’s best to ask a staff member in your ticket what actions you need to take to pursue advancement. Usually, you need to obtain a certain amount of relevant successes after declaring your project and getting approval.

Some Advantages, such as any classified as "Mythic", are only available at character creation.

## **Backgrounds**

Backgrounds are gained similarly to merits but with different caveats depending on the background. Each purchase requires an experience spent in addition to the following successes.

* 1 dot- 15 successes
* 2 dots- 20 successes
* 3 dots- 30 successes
* 4 dots- 75 successes
* 5 dots- 100 successes

### **Allies**

* 1 dot- 15 successes
* 2 dots- 30 successes
* 3 dots- 75 successes
* 4 dots- 100 successes

### **Contacts**

* 1 dot- 15 successes
* 2 dots- 30 successes
* 3 dots- 75 successes

### **Haven**

Players may either use a number of Resource dots to gain a Haven or gain a number of successes to explain the acquisition. This burns the Resources for at least a Story, perhaps longer. The requirements are as follows:

1 dot- 15 ss\
2 dots- 30 ss\
3 dots- 75 ss

#### **Haven Merits**

Players may either use a number of Resource dots or successes to add modifications to their Haven. Use the chart under "Backgrounds" for further guidance.

### **Lore Sheets**

There is no set way to achieve Lore Sheets, as diverse as they are. If there is a Lore Sheet that you want to take, ask a staff member in your ticket, and we will consult with you. Gaining dots 4-5 usually requires planning across multiple Chronicles. Remember that, like other Advantages, each level of a Lore Sheet is self-contained and must be bought individually. It does not automatically give you the “lower” levels of that Lore Sheet. Each level of dot for a Lore Sheet can be bought _out_ of order, meaning you do not have to buy the first, second, and third dots consecutively.

### **Mask**

Players must either have the Cobbler merit or purchase a Mask from a PC Cobbler to craft a Mask.

Working on a Mask will use up the Cobbler's training roll for the night (or one or more of their 10 freebie rolls in Timeskip). Masks can be made for others and must be purchased by the one it’s for by the end of time-skip.

To earn the Cobbler merit in RP, you need to be taught by another Cobbler and score 15 successes in a project, but you don't need a 2-dot Mask. In character creation, the Cobbler merits still follow the rules as written. To craft a Mask, you need 15 successes for the first dot and 30 successes for the second. Zeroed is an additional 15 successes. You must build up to each step of the Mask; getting a level 2 Mask and Zeroed would be 60 total successes, for example. What pool you use depends on how you're doing it, whether it's tech-based or good old-fashioned forgery.
