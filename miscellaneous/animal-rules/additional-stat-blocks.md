# Additional Stat Blocks

Note: The information on this page is for major animals. All minor animals not listed receive the generic stat block as stated below (see below for the full list of minor animals).&#x20;

## Minor Animals

Existing in a subdirectory within the groups of animals in Cape Town, these animals pose either minor risks or no risks at all to the predators and major animals within Cape Town. Shrews, Mice, Gerbils, and all kinds of small animals consist of these minor animals. While small or insignificant, the instant ignorance of these animals can prove to be a decision one may regret.

**Standard Dice Pools:** Physical 2, Social 1, Mental 1

**Secondary Attributes:** Health 2, Willpower 2

**Exceptional Dice Pools:** Athletics 4, Awareness 4

**Special:** Due to their small stature, these creatures are capable of sneaking under small crevices in walls or hiding in plain sight. All Minor Animals can gain access to areas that would be inaccessible for other creatures along with +2 Stealth rolls that involve hiding in plain sight.
