# Sports and Game Rules

{% hint style="warning" %}
As with sparring, to prevent success spamming, you can only make three of these rolls in a scene.
{% endhint %}

### Bowling

**Pool:** Strength + Athletics

* DC 2 to send it down the lane and hit some pins
* 8 successes over two rolls (1 frame) to hit a spare
* Crit on second roll of a frame to guarantee a spare
* Crit on first roll of a frame for a strike
* On fail, it's a gutter ball

### Billiards

**Pool:** Dexterity + Composure

#### The Break

* DC 3. Sinks one ball. Every two additional successes on the margin sinks another ball of your color (stripes / solids).
* Failure: No balls sunk. Enter regular play (stripes / solids determined on first sinking).
* Total failure: Scratch, plus DC 2 Fury Frenzy.
* 12-success crit: Get all balls in the pockets on the break

#### In Play

* DC 3. Sink a ball in a pocket. Every 3 additional successes on the margin sinks an extra ball.
* Total failure: Sink one of your opponent’s balls or scratch (you sunk the cue ball; add one of your balls back to the table). Use /coinflip to determine.

#### The Eight Ball

* DC 4 to sink the Eight Ball in the called pocket. Remember if you do this prematurely, you lose
* Total failure: As above.

#### Messy Crit / Bestial Failure options:

* Clan compulsion (if relevant)
* Dominance compulsion
* Sink a number of the opponent’s balls into pockets (margin / 2, min 2)
* Scratch (possibly DC 2 fury frenzy provocation)
* Sink Eight Ball at wrong time (lose the game)
