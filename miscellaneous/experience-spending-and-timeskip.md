# Experience Spending and Timeskip

{% hint style="info" %}
**The next timeskip** starts on September 11, 2024.
{% endhint %}

## Timeskip

At the end of every Chronicle **(12 IC Nights/Three OOC Months),** CTbN undergoes a timeskip period lasting three weeks. Each character that completes a scene earns 15xp and also receives 10 free project rolls, which can be used to accrue the last remaining successes in a project or complete a new one. Unlike rolls during the chronicle, these rolls are made **without** Hunger dice, and you may surge and spend Willpower on each without cost (just don't click the "Surge" and "Mark WP" buttons when they pop up).

During this period, you may:

* Train Attributes and Skills
* Hunt for a specific Resonance
* Gain new Backgrounds
* Change Convictions

RP during this period is **unlimited**. You can be in as many scenes at once as you like! Players are limited to opening 5 tickets at a time but may join other scenes if another player opens them instead. When the timeskip ends, the in-game date advances by roughly three months.

{% hint style="info" %}
**What's my Hunger? HP? WP?**

Each scene begins with Hunger 1 (you may begin higher if you wish). All Superficial HP and WP damage is healed. You may heal **one Aggravated damage** of both for free.
{% endhint %}

Unused successes fade at the end of timeskip unless they are part of a project to obtain an Advantage, as do any “shadow” dots (Allies, Status, etc. that are gained through events and roleplay) that aren't locked in with experience. For your convenience, you may use this [Google Sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1DwYmWwWIQi6DHwjxgFfZaF-xBVeIRn5D\_XWOTj0k4ps/edit?usp=sharing) to track your successes, Resonances, and planned purchases, though you are not obligated to.

## Spending experience

During timeskip, please provide a list of expenditures and your sheet when you have decided what you wish to purchase, as well as justifications (project rolls, stats command, RP scenes, etc.). Please do not upload your new sheet until you are certain that your purchases are final. **Staff will only do one bulk deduction of experience per character.**

{% hint style="warning" %}
During regular play, each character may make only one purchase. Rules regarding successes and valid RP still apply.
{% endhint %}

### **Advantages**

All merits require some rolls or scenes spent working toward them relative to their dot value; aside from Looks merits, which require Fleshcrafting. The rolls or actions needed to gain merits are different, so it’s best to ask a staff member in your ticket what actions you need to be going for to pursue advancement. Usually you need to obtain a certain amount of relevant successes (after declaring your project and getting approval) and write a vignette to secure it.

Some merits, such as any classified as "Mythic", are only available at chargen.

#### **Backgrounds**

Backgrounds are gained similarly to merits, but with different caveats depending on the background itself. Each purchase requires an experience spend in addition to the following successes.

* 1 dot- 15 successes
* 2 dots- 20 successes
* 3 dots- 30 successes
* 4 dots- 75 successes
* 5 dots- 100 successes

_**Allies**_

* 1 dot- 15 successes
* 2 dots- 30 successes
* 3 dots- 75 successes
* 4 dots- 100 successes

_**Contacts**_

* 1 dot- 15 successes
* 2 dots- 30 successes
* 3 dots- 75 successes

_**Haven**_

Players may either use a number of Resource dots to gain a Haven or gain a number of successes to explain the acquisition. This burns the Resources for at least a Story, perhaps longer. The requirements are as follows:

1 dot- 15 ss\
2 dots- 30 ss\
3 dots- 75 ss

_**Haven Merits**_

Players may either use a number of Resource dots or successes to add modifications to their Haven. Use the chart under "Backgrounds" for further guidance.

_**Loresheets**_

There is no set way to achieve Lore Sheets, as diverse as they are. If there is a Loresheet that you are wanting to take, ask a staff member in your ticket and we will consult with you. Gaining dots 4-5 usually requires planning across the span of multiple Chronicles. Remember that just like other Advantages, each level of a Loresheet is self-contained and must be bought individually. It does not automatically give you the “lower” levels of that Loresheet. Each level of dot for a Loresheet can be bought _out_ of order. Meaning you do not have have to buy the first, second, and third dots consecutively.

_**Mask**_

To craft a Mask, players must either have the Cobbler merit or purchase a Mask from a PC Cobbler. Expect to pay in boons!

Working on a Mask will use up the Cobbler's training roll for the night (or one or more of their 10 freebie rolls in Timeskip). Masks can be made for others and must be purchased by the one it’s for by the end of time-skip.

To earn the Cobbler merit in RP, you need to be taught by another Cobbler and score 15 successes in a project, but you don't need to have a 2-dot Mask. In character creation, the Cobbler merits still follow the rules as written. To craft a Mask, you need 15 successes for the first dot and 30 successes for the second. Zeroed is an additional 15 successes. You must build up to each step of the Mask; getting a level 2 Mask and Zeroed would be 60 successes total, for example. What pool you use depends on how you're doing it, whether it's tech-based or good old-fashioned forgery.

#### <mark style="color:green;">Out-of-clan Disciplines</mark>

To learn the first dot of an out-of-clan Discipline requires drinking the blood of a character who has it in-clan. Vitae need only be drunk once; once the Discipline is unlocked, it is unlocked. It does not need to be taken "from the tap" and may be drank from a cup to avoid the bond; however 'from the vein' counts as the resonance required. Vitae retains its ability to unlock Disciplines for 15 minutes outside of the body. You must then receive training, though the training doesn't need to be from the blood donor.

Subsequent purchases merely require appropriate resonance and temperament, as detailed below.

| Melancholy | Fortitude, Obfuscate, Oblivion      |

### **Rituals, Ceremonies, and Formulae**

Experience is to be spent after research is completed, but before the ability is used. The table below shows the necessary time to be spent studying the material to unlock the abilities with any modifiers noted below. Players are not required to have research scenes and they do not affect study time.

* <mark style="color:yellow;">Level 1: 1 IC week</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Level 2: 2 IC weeks</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Level 3: 5 IC weeks</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Level 4: 10 IC weeks</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">Level 5: 14 IC weeks</mark>

All relevant dots decrease the study time required by one night each. Suitable Library and Occult dots are used for Blood Sorcery and Oblivion while Lab and Science are used for Formulae. Appropriate Mawla and specialties apply to all three.

Players can purchase as many rituals as they have time to study based on the chart above (ie: one level 4 and two level 2 abilities; 14 weeks IC). Time spent during time-skip _does_ count toward the required learning time. You must make note when you begin research on a new ritual, ceremony, or formula. If you do not declare when the research began, you cannot go back and claim lost time; you must start from the current date.

Instructions for level 4 and 5 abilities can only be gained from events or through personal arcs or tasks that the player pursues.\
\
To learn a ritual or ceremony out-of-clan, you require a teacher with enough dots in the relevant Discipline to learn it themselves. The teacher does not need to know the exact ritual you want to learn, however, and this rule does not bypass the need for an arc for level 4/5 rituals.\
\
&#x20;Rituals do not use up your in-chronicle purchase.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Cult-specific** level 4 and 5 Ceremonies, Rituals, and powers require cult membership.
{% endhint %}

### Humanity Increases

<mark style="color:yellow;">The cost of purchasing humanity is 2x the new humanity level. If you want to buy humanity you must meet the following requirements:</mark>

* <mark style="color:yellow;">You cannot have gained stains, succumbed to frenzy, or rolled bestial failures/messy criticals in the past two nights.</mark>
* <mark style="color:yellow;">You must have acted selflessly and with compassion, showing you can be more humane. Touchstone scenes do not count for this purpose. Links must be provided to show an active effort being made.</mark>

<mark style="color:yellow;">You can apply for a humanity increase once per chronicle and it does not use up your in chronicle purchase. You cannot go up more than one humanity per chronicle.</mark>

### Blood Potency Increases

<mark style="color:yellow;">Increasing Blood Potency can only be done during a chronicle where a character experiences events that would thicken their blood, such as; killing to sate their beast, repeated exposure to vitae, allowing their Beast free reign by riding the wave, or dia</mark>blerie.

Actively pursuing a Blood Potency increase requires the character to know that it is something they can do in the first place. Criteria and methods are listed below:

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Knowledge Criteria" %}
* Ancilla: Your character may automatically know the methods for increasing Blood Potency.
* Neonate: Your character needs 2 dots in Occult or a Mawla that is an Ancilla or older. Otherwise, make an Intelligence + Occult roll at DC 4.
* Fledgling: Make an Intelligence + Occult roll at DC 4.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Methods" %}
* **Killing:** Drain a number of humans equal to twice the desired blood potency (once per night).
* **Vitae exposure:** Feed on higher-level vitae for a number of nights equal to twice the desired blood potency. This must be from the vein, creating a bond.
* **Riding the wave:** Give into frenzy a number of times equal to twice the desired blood potency (once per night).
* **Diablerie** works the same as written in the core book.
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}

All events must take place during regular RP, not time skip, and must happen on consecutive nights. You can apply for a Blood Potency increase once per chronicle, and it does not use up your in-chronicle purchase. You cannot go up more than once per chronicle. You may only choose one method from the list above- they cannot be mixed and matched. Characters may only perform one "step" per night.
