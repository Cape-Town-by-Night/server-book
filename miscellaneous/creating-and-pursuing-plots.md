# Creating and Pursuing Plots

In a sandbox-style server, most plot points you pursue will be cemented in personal character arcs as opposed to those set up by Storytellers. Players are always free to pursue their arcs and assist in the resolution of others’ while staff offers guidance for the mechanical aspect of play. For example, you have complete control over characters in your PC’s backgrounds unless your character is gaining some benefit (mechanical or otherwise) from the interaction. At that point, you would ask staff for assistance and permission. A great place to ask for help with this is in your ticket or (if it is an arc with multiple characters involved) your coordination thread with your partners.

Staff are always happy to answer questions—in fact, we welcome them! However, you must be the driving force behind your character’s arcs and decisions. In a larger server, such as this one, staff cannot give as much individual attention to players while remaining fair to everyone. If you’re stuck on what to do, there is a short list on page 138 of the core book to give you a jumpstart.

Please bear in mind that the player is expected to have a general idea of what they want to do and how they’d like to go about it. It is best to state both of these things at once to minimize any unnecessary back and forth. Also worth noting is that plots will not always resolve in a way that is beneficial to your character. Sometimes they will stumble to a conclusion or the thread will end before they can get a meaningful foothold and contribute.

Say your character is an average street thug that wants to move up to “kingpin” status. You could achieve this by doing projects for things like Criminal Allies, Contacts, Underworld Influence, etc. In addition, you could partner up with other players whose characters share similar traits and connections in an attempt to make allies (or enemies). You could also get involved with SPCs that are aligned with your goals as Storyteller time allows, either to work with them or to attempt to usurp them. And if your character isn’t someone who makes elaborate plots to get what they want? Are they someone who hasn’t entrenched themselves into the city yet? That’s totally fine, as well. You can scout out player characters who are and get involved with them. Plots tend to come around naturally, especially those attached to Advantages and Flaws.
