In addition to the questions and answers below, be sure to check the [rulings](miscellaneous/rulings/) page and our [Advantages and Flaws](https://cape-town-by-night.gitbook.io/copy-ct-wiki/\~/changes/Zpb0Yfi4wmYUaSKQqNcs/getting-started/character-creation#advantage-restrictions-and-changes) changes.

_**How do I spend Experience?**_

There is a detailed experience spending guide [here](experience-spending.md).

_**"What kinds of things do I need to submit a scene request for?"**_ Any interaction with an SPC requires a scene request. The player can run touchstones and any background characters related to Advantages and Flaws as a vignette. Care should be taken not to treat these as tertiary characters. They should interact with other PCs as little as possible.

_**"There are some areas with no hunting difficulty noted. What do I use?"**_

The standard difficulty in V5 is three. Use that unless instructed otherwise.

_**"I got a messy critical/bestial failure on my roll. What do I do?"**_ \
\
Ping both the <mark style="color:purple;">@Storyteller</mark> and the <mark style="color:blue;">@Assistant Storyteller</mark> roles in the channel with your result and give context for what is happening in the scene. We'll tailor the results to the situation on a case-by-case scenario. Remember to try to reroll with Willpower using the "Avoid Messy" or "Reroll Failures" buttons before pinging, should you wish to do that. \
\
Keep in mind that there is a ten-minute time limit before the re-roll buttons get greyed out.

#### _**"Do Banu Haqim have to diablerize a kindred victim if they fail their Bane check?"**_

No. They will simply feed until their victim reaches Hunger 5 or they reach Hunger 1. You may always choose to diablerize, of course, but make sure you ping staff to oversee the situation.

#### _**"How much can a Kindred feed from a mortal? What happens if they take more?"**_

A kindred can slake a maximum of 2 Hunger from a single mortal (and far, far less from animals) before endangering them. Each slake requires 2 stories to recover for a regular mortal and a single story per slake for a ghoul. \
\
A mortal who has been fed on for 3 or more Hunger in a single feeding must make a Strength + Stamina roll (DC equals the amount of Hunger slaked) to stay alive. If they fail this roll, they die. If they succeed, they will survive the blood loss but will need to seek medical treatment.

#### _**"Help! My character took too much from a mortal and they are dying!"**_

Mortals are fragile creatures. They can be stabilized and hospitalized if they succeed on their Strength + Stamina roll.

### Weapons

#### _**"How do I obtain a firearm?"**_

Legally, you would need someone to go out during the day to file any paperwork and renew the license. The gun would not be in your character's name unless they were the one going out and doing those things themselves. A retainer can be used for this purpose. Resources are also required. Illegally, you would seek out appropriate criminal contacts and use them to get weapons. Resources would also be required for this method. The specific amount of dots needed depends on the weapon desired.

### Coteries and Domains

#### _**"How do I form a coterie?"**_

Gather the intended coterie members together, between 3-7 kindred. For coordination purposes, you may create a [group ticket](https://discord.gg/tFqVDZVmXn) and add the other members to it. From there, use the coterie creation rules (Core p. 195)

### Mask

#### _**"How do I make a Mask for someone?"**_

You need a 2 dot Mask in order to get the Cobbler merit at character creation. You need to gain 15 successes for the first dot and 30 successes for the second. Pools vary based on your character's methods. You can only work on one Mask at a time and doing so eats your project/training roll for the night. Once the Mask is finished, the recipient must pay for the Merit using experience by the end of the next time skip. Otherwise, it becomes unusable.

Common rolls for Mask making are Resolve + Larceny/Craft/Technology with relevant specialties added.

#### _"How do I get the Cobbler merit in RP?"_

To earn the Cobbler merit in RP, you need to be taught by another Cobbler and score 15 successes in a project, but you don't need to have a 2-dot Mask.

In character creation, the Cobbler merits still follows the rules as written_._&#x20;

### Using Recording Devices

Recording devices on your person must be marked in your header. You may review (in-character) up to three scenes per IC night. Each review consumes one of your hunting chances.

### Drugs

#### _"How can I find drugs?"_

To find various drugs, you must make a successful Charisma+Streetwise roll. The DC depends on the type of drug desired:

* **Alcohol:** DC 2
* **Cocaine/Meth/Speed:** DC 5
* **Hallucinogens:** DC 4
* **Heroin/Morphine/Oxy/Opiates:** DC 5
* **Marijuana:** DC 3

On a total failure, your character is poisoned.&#x20;

#### _"Can my Herd give me drug effects?"_

Yes. You forfeit your ability to gain Resonance from that Herd, but may select one type of drug to affect them instead. This can be changed at the same frequency as resonance for that level of herd.

### Thin-Blood Alchemy and Resonance

p.283 dictates: "The specific amount \[of resonant blood] needed \[for Thin-Blood Alchemy] depends on the degree of the donor’s Resonance and on the Storyteller’s whim."

We use the following guidlines:\
\
**Negligible:** No Resonance possible \
**Fleeting:** 2 Hunger's worth required \
**Intense:** 1 Hunger's worth required \
**Acute:** 1/2 Hunger's worth required

### The Player's Guide

#### _"Is X allowed?"_

[players-guide-material](book-information/players-guide-material/ "mention")

#### _"Why is X banned?"_

Many items in the Player's Guide are unsuited for play-by-post. Some require too much ST fiat for a game with close to 100 characters; others are simply unbalanced for our style of play.

#### _"Why not homebrew something to fit better instead of banning it?"_

We try to keep powers and advantages as close to RAW as possible. Not only is this easier for us, but it's also easier for players who may come from or eventually join another game.
