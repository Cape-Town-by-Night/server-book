## **I got a messy critical/bestial failure on my roll. What do I do?**

Ping the <mark style="color:purple;">@Storyteller</mark> and the <mark style="color:blue;">@Assistant Storyteller</mark> roles in the channel with your result and give context for what is happening in the scene. We'll tailor the results to the situation on a case-by-case scenario. Remember to try to reroll with Willpower using the "Avoid Messy" or "Reroll Failures" buttons before pinging, should you wish to do that. \
\
Remember that there is a ten-minute time limit before the re-roll buttons get greyed out.

## **Will Banu Haqim have to diablerize a Kindred if they fail their Bane check?**

No. They will simply feed until their victim reaches Hunger 5 or they reach Hunger 1. You may always choose to diablerize, but ensure you ping <mark style="color:blue;">@Staff</mark> to oversee the situation.

## **How much can a Kindred feed from a mortal? What happens if they take more?**

A kindred can slake a maximum of 2 Hunger from a single mortal (and far, far less from animals) before endangering them. Each slake requires 2 stories to recover for a regular mortal and a single story per slake for a ghoul. \
\
A mortal who has been fed on for 3 or more Hunger in a single feeding must make a Strength + Stamina roll (DC equals the amount of Hunger slaked) to stay alive. If they fail this roll, they die. If they succeed, they will survive the blood loss but will need to seek medical treatment.

## **How do mortals heal from feedings?**

Kindred inflict harmful drinks on mortals when they either slake more than 3 hunger in a single feeding or feed while in frenzy. This inflicts a number of Aggravated damage equal to Hunger slaked. The mortal must then make a Strength + Stamina test at a Difficulty equal to the number of slakes taken. If they fail this roll, the mortal dies. If they pass, they can be safely transported to a medical facility where they can heal properly. Their wounds can be healed if they survive, per p.127 in the core book.

## **How do I form a coterie?**

Gather the intended coterie members together, between 3-7 kindred. You may create a group ticket and add the other members for coordination. From there, use the coterie creation rules (Core p. 195)

## **How do I make a Mask?**

You need a 2 dot Mask to get the Cobbler merit at character creation. You need to gain 15 successes for the first dot and 30 successes for the second. Pools vary based on your character's methods. You can only work on one Mask at a time, and doing so uses your project/training roll for the night. Once the Mask is finished, the recipient must pay for the Merit using experience by the end of the next time skip. Otherwise, it becomes unusable.

Common rolls for Mask making are Resolve + Larceny/Craft/Technology with relevant specialties added.

### Gaining the Cobbler Merit

To earn the Cobbler merit in RP, you need to be taught by another Cobbler and score 15 successes in a project, but you don't need a 2-dot Mask.

At character creation, the Cobbler merits still follow the rules as written_._&#x20;

## How do I use recording devices?

Recording devices on your person must be marked in your header. You may review (in-character) up to three scenes per IC night. Each review consumes one of your hunting chances.

## How do I find drugs?

You must make a successful Charisma + Streetwise roll to find various drugs. The DC depends on the type of drug desired:

* **Alcohol:** DC 2
* **Cocaine/Meth/Speed:** DC 5
* **Hallucinogens:** DC 4
* **Heroin/Morphine/Oxy/Opiates:** DC 5
* **Marijuana:** DC 3

On a total failure, your character is poisoned.&#x20;

## Can my Herd foster a drug type?

Yes. You forfeit your ability to gain Resonance from that Herd, but may select one type of drug to affect them instead. This can be changed at the same frequency as resonance for that level of Herd.

## Thin-Blood Alchemy and Resonance

In the core book, p.283 dictates: "The specific amount \[of resonant blood] needed \[for Thin-Blood Alchemy] depends on the degree of the donor’s Resonance and the Storyteller’s whim."

We use the following guidelines:\
\
**Negligible:** No Resonance possible \
**Fleeting:** 2 Hunger's worth required \
**Intense:** 1 Hunger's worth required \
**Acute:** 1/2 Hunger's worth required
