# Progression Caps

To maintain fairness and prevent server stagnation, we enforce limits on Attributes, Skills, Disciplines, and experience amounts. These caps are crucial for all players to have a balanced, enjoyable gaming experience.

## Attribute Caps

* Fledglings- One 5 dot, two 4 dots, three 3 dots, three 2 dots
* Neonates- One 5 dot, three 4 dots, four 3 dots, one 2 dot
* Ancilla- Two 5 dots, three 4 dots, four 3 dots

## Skill Caps

* Fledglings- One 5 dots, two 4 dots, four 3 dots, eight 2 dots, no cap on 1 dots.
* Neonates- Two 5 dots, three 4 dots, six 3 dots, no cap on 2 dots or less.
* Ancilla- Three 5 dots, four 4 dots, no cap on 3 or less.

Once your character has hit their respective Attribute and Skill caps, you may not increase these stats further. However, you may decrease current Attributes and Skills to increase others. This is to keep characters from feeling stagnant once they reach their peak.

## Discipline Caps

All characters are restricted to a maximum of 6 different Disciplines.

* Fledglings- Level 5 inaccessible, one level 4, two level 3, no other caps.
* Neonates- One level 5, two level 4, no other caps.
* Ancilla- Three level 5, two level 4, no other caps.

## Experience Caps

Characters cannot gain experience past their generational cap, as listed below. Experience gained at character creation (excluding retirement) is not a part of this total. The amounts are based on character age and are as follows:

* Mortals: 350
* Fledgling/ghoul: 400
* Neonate: 450
* Ancilla: 500
