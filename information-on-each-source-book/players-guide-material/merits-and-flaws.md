# Merits & Flaws

## General Advantages

### Merits

#### Allowed

* Ingénue (Looks)
* Vessel Recognition (Feeding)
* Nuit Mode (Mythic)
* Check the Trunk (Other)

#### Banned

* Famous Face (Looks)
* Remarkable Feature (Looks)
* Luck of the Devil (Mythic)
* Side Hustler (Other)
* Tempered Will (Other)
* Untouchable (Other)
* City Secrets (Status)

### Flaws

#### Allowed

* Stench (Looks)
* Transparent (Looks)
* Starving Decay (Mythic)
* Twice Cursed (Mythic)
* Knowledge Hungry (Other)
* Weak-willed (Other)

#### Banned

* Vein Tapper (Feeding)
* Prestation Debts (Other)
* Risk-taker (Other)

#### Banned

## Caitiff Merits and Flaws

### Allowed

* Muddled Blood (Flaw)

### Banned

All other Caitiff merits and flaws are banned.

## Haven Merits and Flaws

### Merits

#### Allowed

* Business Establishment
* Machine Shop
* Furcus- The Furcus can be detected with an Intelligence + Occult check, DC 6 - Furcus rating. Sense the Unseen and Oblivion's Sight may also detect the Furcus with the same roll.

### Flaws

#### Allowed

* Shared
