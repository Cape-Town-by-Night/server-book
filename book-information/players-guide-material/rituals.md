description: >-
  The Player's Guide adds several new rituals, both for Blood Sorcery and for
  Thin-Blood Alchemy. As not all of them are suitable to our setting, below is
  the list of what has been approved or banned.
---

# Rituals

## Blood Sorcery Rituals

### Allowed

#### Level 2

* As Fog on Water
* Calix Secretus

#### Level 4

* Feasts of Ashes

#### Level 5

* Dominion

### Reprinted

#### Using original version

* Douse the Fear (Level 1) _(Cults of the Blood Gods)_
* Soporific Touch (Level 2) _(Camarilla)_

### Banned

* Seal the Brand (Level 1)
* Guided Memory (Level 4)
* Invisible Chains of Binding (Level 4)

## Thin-Blood Alchemy

### Allowed

#### Level 1

* Mercurian Tongue
* Plug-In

#### Level 2

* Friends List

#### Level 3

* Mandagloire
* Rumor
* Tank

#### Level 4

* Short Circuit
* Toxic Personality

#### Level 5

* Moment of Clarity

### Banned

* Flowering Amaranth (Level 5)
