# Predator Types

<details>

<summary><strong>Alleycat (Core pp.175-176)</strong></summary>

<sup>_A combative assault-feeder, you stalk, overpower, and drink from whomever you can, when you can. You may or may not attempt to threaten or Dominate victims into silence or mask the feeding as a robbery. Think about how you arrived at this direct approach to feeding and what makes you comfortable with an unlife of stalking, attacking, feeding, and escaping. You could have been homeless, an SAS soldier, a cartel hit-man, or a big-game hunter._</sup>

**Specialty:** Intimidation _(Stickups)_ or Brawl _(Grappling)_

**Discipline Choice:** [<mark style="color:$danger;">Celerity</mark>](powers/#celerity) or [<mark style="color:$danger;">Potence</mark>](powers/#potence)

**Merits:** Three dots of criminal [<mark style="color:$danger;">Contacts</mark>](advantages-and-flaws/#contacts)

**Flaws:** Lose one dot of Humanity

**Pool:** _Strength + Brawl or Wits + Streetwise_

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Bagger (Core p.176)</strong></summary>

<sub>_You steal, buy, or otherwise procure cold blood rather than hunt, relying on the black market or your skills as a burglar or ambulance chaser. Perhaps you still work the night shift at the hospital. Ventrue may not pick this Predator type._</sub>

**Specialty:** Larceny (Lockpicking) or Streetwise (Black Market)

**Discipline Choice:** [Blood Sorcery](powers/#blood-sorcery) (Tremere, Banu Haqim, or Koldun only) or [Obfuscate](powers/#obfuscate)

**Merits:** [Iron Gullet](advantages-and-flaws/#iron-gullet-core-p.182)

**Flaws:** Two dots of [Enemies](advantages-and-flaws/#enemies-core-pp.184-185)

**Pool:** _Intelligence + Streetwise_&#x20;

</details>

<summary><mark style="color:red;"><strong>Cleaver (Core pp.176-177)</strong></mark></summary>

_You feed covertly from your (or&#x20;someone’s) mortal family and&#x20;friends with whom you still maintain ties. The most extreme cleavers&#x20;adopt children, marry a human,&#x20;and try to maintain a family life&#x20;for as long as they can._

_Cleavers often go to great&#x20;lengths to keep the truth of their&#x20;condition from their family, but&#x20;some also maintain unwholesome&#x20;relationships with their own kin.&#x20;The Camarilla forbids taking a&#x20;human family in this fashion, and it frowns on cleavers as Masquerade breaches waiting&#x20;to happen. Wiser Kindred may massacre your family&#x20;for your own good if they find out your secret and care&#x20;what happens to you._

**Specialty:** Persuasion (_Gaslighting_) or Subterfuge (_Coverups_)

**Discipline Choice:** [Dominate](powers/#dominate) or [Animalism](powers/#animalism)&#x20;

**Merits:** Two dots of [Herd](advantages-and-flaws/#herd-core-pp.189-190)

**Flaws:** Dark Secret, [Cleaver](advantages-and-flaws/#dark-secret-core-p.187)&#x20;

**Pool:** _Manipulation + Subterfuge_

</details>
