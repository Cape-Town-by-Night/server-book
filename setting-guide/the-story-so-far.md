# The Story So Far

It wasn't a century ago that the City of Cape Town was firmly within the clutches of the Camarilla. None dared speak against Prince Michael, a veteran of the Great War - nor his court - lest they receive a visit from the Scourge. But times, like most things, change...&#x20;

It was the summer of '62 when the Twins - Felix and Tristitia, a pair of differing Blood but unified goals, decided to stand up to the Tower of Cape Town. Using the political tension and clashes during the Apartheid regime as a smokescreen to keep the Kine unaware, the pair waged a war against the Camarilla, bringing a large section of the City Centre and the Central Suburbs under the Flag of the Movement. In early 2006, a Malkavian named Jason came forth with a vision - a city in flames at the hands of the Kindred that resided within. A warning heeded by most until recently.&#x20;

Seeing the disaster this could bring and unwilling to risk testing the truth behind the vision, a treaty was negotiated between the two Sects. Prince Michael disappeared - some said went underground - and Praxis was seized by the Ventrue Rorke, a tyrant who ruled with an iron fist. But her grip was not tight enough.&#x20;

During the height of Rorke’s reign, a plurality of Masquerade breaches by the Camarilla led to a crackdown on the city’s Kindred by the agents of the Second Inquisition. From threatening Kindred outright to cracking down on their financial accounts, this purge culminated when a troublesome Anarch Bahari, Narcissa, was captured by the Inquisition and forced to give the location of Elysium under the threat of meeting the sun once more. Under cover of antiterrorist action, the Inquisition set the Elysium aflame, killing many of the Tower’s leadership and scattering the sect to the wind for a brief time.&#x20;

Following this attack, the Camarilla managed to regroup and rebuild. At a meeting called to all Tower Kindred who would come to Skeleton Gorge, Prince Rorke attempted to reassert her Praxis. Many Kindred rallied against her, citing her complacency for the Camarilla’s ruin. Revealing herself to be a Fae in disguise, Rorke issued an ultimatum to let the humans prosper or be plunged into a war with the Fae. After a brief clash, the Tower retreated, and the Nosferatu Daisy, who had shown mettle, seized Praxis.&#x20;

Over the next few years, the burgeoning Camarilla faced problems, such as a Shalimite cult taking root in the city and the indiscriminate killings of Narcissa (also known as Noube), which caused the Inquisition to once again cast its gaze upon Cape Town, but all were overcome. The Tower and the Movement to the North continued to gain strength in peacetime. Having learned their lesson from Narcissa, the location of Elysium was a closely guarded secret.

Of course, peace was tested at times. Baron Ruiveshna attempted to clandestinely gather Thin-Bloods to her banner to mount an attack on the Tower but was slain by a cultist assassin before this could happen. The Gangrel Valery, a loyal dog of the tower, attempted to seize the Docks held by the Movement but was thwarted when his plot was revealed and was undone by his own Prince to ensure that the peace was kept. The Inquisition, too, remained a silent and looming threat.&#x20;

Lasombra Graham planned and executed a cross-sect operation to kill Inquisition soldiers, but the operation ended in disaster when the Inquisition rallied, and all but a lucky few lost their lives. For months, the Inquisition scoured the city, seeking revenge against the Kindred who had hunkered down, but they found few leads of their own to pursue.&#x20;

In the time of relative quiet that followed, a defector from the Camarilla who had aspirations of Anarch membership let slip the location of the Camarilla’s Elysium - Steenburg Vineyards - to a rash group of Anarchs. In an attack unsanctioned by their sect, an oil tanker was driven through the Steenburg Hotel’s gate, tearing apart the tenuous peace and once again reigniting the flames of conflict.&#x20;

Unwilling to back down and lose face lest they look weak to those who would try to steal their seats from under them, the Prince and the Barony have both declared war on the other. Though some Kindred will try to go about their unlives as normal, the future of Cape Town is uncertain and in the hands of those Kindred who brave the dangers of the night to leave their mark on it.
