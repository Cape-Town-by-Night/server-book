# Convictions and Touchstones

### Convictions

Convictions are a set of beliefs that assist in defining a character’s worldview and morals. Each conviction must be linked to a living human, or “touchstone”, that exemplifies the belief itself. Touchstones must be living in Cape Town and can be backgrounds gained from advantages (Contacts, Allies, etc.).&#x20;

Convictions are placed into categories depending on their importance to the kindred who holds them; defining, major, and minor. The more significant a conviction is to the kindred, the more protection it grants their humanity when it is upheld. On the flip side, violating a more important conviction will also incur more stains. The stain amounts for either situation are as follows:&#x20;

* **Defining:** +/- 3 stains
  * _Core beliefs, ethics, and perspectives. Something the character carries close as often and consistently as possible._
* **Major:** +/- 2 stains
  * _Strong outlooks, morals and principles. Ideals that the character sees as important and worth fighting for._
* **Minor:** +/- 1 stain
  * _Loosely established opinions, views, and codes of conduct. Things that they are inclined to believe, but that can quickly fall by the wayside when the going gets tough._

### Touchstone interactions

Characters may heal Aggravated Willpower damage through a touchstone interaction vignette.

To heal willpower damage, characters must make a roll with a difficulty equal to the character’s current aggravated willpower damage. The base DC is 3 until the character has 3 Aggravated Willpower damage, increasing by one from there. (2 Agg WP damage is DC 3, 4 damage is DC 4, etc.)

The pool may be chosen by the player, based on what type of interaction they wish to pursue, but must be approved by staff before rolling. Success restores a single point of Aggravated Willpower damage; a critical restores two. A failure results in no willpower restoration. Bestial failures and messy criticals require Storyteller intervention. These interactions should somehow reflect or embody the Conviction tied to the touchstone!

While touchstone vignettes do not require a Storyteller (unless there's some extra complication to resolve, as above), you **must** receive approval for the interaction first, including the pool in use and the general vibe of the interaction.

Once the vignette is posted, link to it in your ticket for staff to read. Staff will either approve or deny the willpower regen.

#### **What if my Touchstone dies?**

The results of this are completely dependent on _how_ your character's Touchstone died. Catastrophic events and violent deaths damage and destroy Touchstones, which results in Stains (Core p.240).&#x20;

Should your Touchstone die peacefully, you may attempt to transfer the Conviction with a DC 4 Humanity test while meeting with or observing the Touchstone.

If a Touchstone is destroyed and the Conviction is not successfully transferred as detailed above, that Conviction is lost.

#### **What if all of my Touchstones are gone?**

Nothing. Not directly, anyway. Your character will not, however, have any Convictions to rely upon should they earn Stains.&#x20;
