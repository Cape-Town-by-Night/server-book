# Do I Need the Book?

Yes! While we are happy to host newcomers to the game, all players must have at least the Core book. If you are playing a more niche concept that's expanded on in another sourcebook, you will be asked to obtain it.&#x20;

There are many places to buy V5 books, but if you're looking for electronic copies, we recommend [<mark style="color:red;">DriveThruRPG</mark>](https://www.drivethrurpg.com/product/256795/vampire-the-masquerade-5th-edition) (PDF) or the [<mark style="color:red;">V5 Nexus</mark>](https://app.demiplane.com/nexus/vampire) (web-based). Please make sure your copy is up to date!
