# ❌ Common Mechanics

## Using the Dice Bot

Once you're approved, you will need to add your character to Inconnu, which only takes a minute or two. Check the [documentation](https://docs.inconnu.app/guides/quickstart), which will guide you through the process. The documentation also goes over all of Inconnu's other features and how to use them.

## Messy Criticals and Bestial Failures

If you suffer a messy critical or a bestial failure, you may usually click the Re-roll Failures or Avoid Messy buttons on the roll, which might help you escape complications. Be sure to click Mark WP afterward. If you can't or don't want to avoid the complication, ping <mark style="color:purple;">@Storyteller</mark> for instructions.

Only ping if you're keeping the outcome! There's no need to ask if you're allowed to re-roll. The option will only appear if you can do so.

## **Awakening and Blush**

After the weekly reset, post an awakening vignette. All vampires, including Thin-Bloods, must perform a wake-up Rouse check (use the `/awaken` command). For those who need to, rolling Blush of Life (`/bol`) is optional. If your character is staying inside your haven or sneaking around places that mortals don't tend to go (the sewers, a cave system, etc), there is no need to Blush. If your character intends on going to locations that mortals frequent at night, Blush is all but required to maintain the Masquerade. Heavy cover clothing (hoodies, face masks, gloves, sunglasses) can help, but should only be used if your character's chances of being spotted are small and brief. These outfits will typically attract attention if used when going to the supermarket, for example.

## Slaking Hunger

Use `/slake` to reduce your Hunger. If you are at Hunger 4 or 5, be sure to click the frenzy check button that pops up and ping <mark style="color:purple;">@Storyteller</mark> if you fail.

* Bagged Blood: 2 that may be slaked later on, but needs to be refrigerated within 30 minutes.
* Corpse: Up to 3, slakes as bagged blood. A body already fed from or missing parts will slake less.
* Human: Up to 2 safely.
* Vampire: Up to 5, each Hunger slaked increases the Hunger of the kindred being fed from.

## Mending Damage

Superficial health damage can be healed with the `/mend` command. The `/aggheal` command heals aggravated health damage. These commands (and many others) take your character's Blood Potency into account. They will also make the appropriate amount of rouse checks.

## Hunting

Hunting and hunger management are central themes of V5. Each character gets three hunt attempts per night. They may use this either for themselves or to benefit another character.\
\
Standard hunt difficulties are noted in the corresponding key locations channel. If there is no difficulty noted, use a difficulty of three plus any modifiers. You can check [#map-and-breach-level](https://discord.com/channels/676333549720174605/844179432541126666) for any current hunt difficulty increases.

A critical win on the hunt roll allows your character to get a resonance level one higher than what they rolled.

You will note in the [Server-Specific Information](server-specific-information.md) that hunting vignettes can earn you one experience per week. You must link this in your experience request to get credit for it.

## Social Situations



<mark style="color:red;">The vast majority of interaction between characters is social interaction. While the rulebook describes a number of dice systems for handling social scenarios, players are encouraged to set the dice aside and rely on role-play instead. Not everything has to be a roll, and it's far more satisfying to write out a long con than roll and say it's done.</mark>

<mark style="color:red;">That said, for times when players want to roll, or when rolls are necessary, the following guidelines are in effect.</mark>

## <mark style="color:red;">Persuasion/Seduction</mark>

<mark style="color:red;">No roll outcome can force a PC to act against their convictions or otherwise out of character. Dice rolls can only take you so far. The silver-tongued shyster you met on the street can't force you to give away your life savings just because he scored a critical. The seductive woman at the bar can't make a gay man sleep with her just because she rolled well. And so on.</mark>

<mark style="color:red;">Your character should still react to the roll in some way, even if they don't go along with the proposal. Wow, this man is slick. I bet he's conned a lot of people is perfectly acceptable and still acknowledges the number of successes without violating player agency.</mark>

## <mark style="color:red;">Social Combat</mark>

<mark style="color:red;">We generally discourage using the social combat rules on p.304 in player-vs.-player situations. While it can work well in a table setting, there are many variables that affect damage modifiers, standing, social status, and whether or not social combat should even apply. If both characters are interested in engaging in it, please ping staff.</mark>
