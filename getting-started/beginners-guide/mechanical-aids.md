# Mechanical Aids

## Using the Dice Bot

Once you're approved, you will need to add your character to Inconnu, which only takes a minute or two. Check the [documentation](https://docs.inconnu.app/guides/quickstart), which will guide you through the process. The documentation also goes over all of Inconnu's other features and how to use them.

## Messy Criticals and Bestial Failures

If you suffer a messy critical or a bestial failure, you may usually click the Re-roll Failures or Avoid Messy buttons on the roll, which might help you escape complications. Be sure to click Mark WP afterward. If you can't or don't want to avoid the complication, ping <mark style="color:purple;">@Storyteller</mark> for instructions.

Only ping you're keeping the outcome! There's no need to ask if you're allowed to re-roll. The option will only appear if you can do so.

### Rouse Checks

**Awakening and Blush**

After the weekly reset, post an awakening vignette. All vampires, including Thin-Bloods, must perform a wake-up Rouse check (use the `/awaken` command). For those who need to, rolling Blush of Life (`/bol`) is optional. If your character is staying inside your haven or sneaking around places that mortals don't tend to go (the sewers, a cave system, etc), there is no need to Blush. If your character intends on going to locations that mortals frequent at night, Blush is all but required to maintain the Masquerade. Heavy cover clothing (hoodies, face masks, gloves, sunglasses) can help, but should only be used if your character's chances of being spotted are small and brief. These outfits will typically attract attention if used when going to the supermarket, for example.

#### Slaking Hunger

Use `/slake` to reduce your Hunger. If you are at Hunger 4 or 5, be sure to click the frenzy check button that pops up and ping <mark style="color:purple;">@Storyteller</mark> if you fail.

* Bagged Blood: 2 that may be slaked later on, but needs to be refrigerated within 30 minutes.
* Corpse: Up to 3, slakes as bagged blood. A body already fed from or missing parts will slake less.
* Human: Up to 2 safely.
* Vampire: Up to 5, each Hunger slaked increases the Hunger of the kindred being fed from.

The vast majority of interaction between characters is social interaction. While the rulebook describes a number of dice systems for handling social scenarios, players are encouraged to set the dice aside and rely on role-play instead. Not everything has to be a roll, and it's far more satisfying to write out a long con than roll and say it's done.

That said, for times when players want to roll, or when rolls are necessary, the following guidelines are in effect.

#### Mending Damage

Use /mend to heal Superficial Health damage at a rate based on your Blood Potency.

Use /aggheal to heal Aggravated Health damage.

Both commands make the appropriate Rouse checks.

You may hunt three times per night. This includes successful hunts, failed hunts, and efforts to assist others in hunting.

For your hunt to count for XP, you must post a vignette narrating your character’s approach, including the results of the dice roll. The maximum amount of Hunger that you can safely slake from a single human is 2. Standard hunting difficulties are noted in the corresponding key locations channels. Check #map-and-breach-levels for any active hunt DC modifiers.

Should you fail your hunt roll, trying again in the same area increases the Difficulty by 1.

If you crit on the hunt roll, you may obtain two vessels or increase the temperament of the blood you receive by one step. For Baggers, you may obtain an extra bag (for a total of 3, not 4). If given the green light by staff, copy+paste -gangrel-4-prez in your ticket if you are playing a Kindred. Copy+paste -vote-no-gangrel if playing Kine. Include the leading hyphen.

When you make your XP request, linking your hunt or awakening scene gives you 1xp.

#### Lying

To check if a character is lying, roll Wits + Insight. The other character rolls Manipulation + Subterfuge if actively lying or Composure + Subterfuge if trying not to reveal something (think "gotta keep my cool"). Ties go to the player who rolled Insight.

#### Persuasion/Seduction

No roll outcome can force a PC to act against their convictions or otherwise out of character. Dice rolls can only take you so far. The silver-tongued shyster you met on the street can't force you to give away your life savings just because he scored a critical. The seductive woman at the bar can't make a gay man sleep with her just because she rolled well. And so on.

Your character should still react to the roll in some way, even if they don't go along with the proposal. Wow, this man is slick. I bet he's conned a lot of people is perfectly acceptable and still acknowledges the number of successes without violating player agency.

#### Intimidation

Go with what makes sense for your character. Even if your character doesn't have the best social traits, someone who has fought against Lupines in the past and lived to tell about it probably won't be frightened by the skinny waif they met on the street. On the other hand, someone might be a physical juggernaut but quake in the presence of sorcery.

As above, acknowledge good (or bad!) rolls in your post, even if your character isn't intimidated.

#### Social Combat

We generally discourage using the social combat rules on p.304 in player-vs.-player situations. While it can work well in a table setting, there are many variables that affect damage modifiers, standing, social status, and whether or not social combat should even apply. If both characters are interested in engaging in it, please ping staff.

#### Experience Requests

Experience is given every Wednesday. Players may earn a maximum of 5xp per week.

Roleplay experience is given automatically, but if you wish to get credit for your desire/ambition and awakening/hunting scenes, you must place a request in #exp-request using the template provided in the channel pin.

#### Breakdown

* 1-3xp for active RP (1 granted once each day you post)
* 1xp for an awakening or hunting vignette. You must link the scenes in your XP request for these to count
* 1xp for meaningfully pursuing your character's desire or ambition

Desire must be posted in your ticket within 24 hours of your awakening post in order for it to count!
