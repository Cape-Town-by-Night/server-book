# Server-Specific Information

## Server Reset

The staff begins resetting the server every Tuesday at 8 PM UTC. After this time, only characters that are mortals, ghouls, or day-drinker thin-bloods may begin new scenes.&#x20;

Players who would like extra time to finish their ongoing scene may post a link in [#st-coordination](https://discord.com/channels/676333549720174605/880104681832063076) along with the word "bubble". You will have until Wednesday at 8 PM UTC to wrap your scene up. Scenes may not continue into the next week except for Storyteller scenes.&#x20;

If you need to hunt or use Herd during reset time but before the beginning of the next in-character night, you may request to do so in your ticket. Any request after the new night has begun for previous weeks will be denied.

## Experience Requests

Experience is given out on Wednesday between 4 and 8 PM UTC. Experience requests may be posted anytime outside of the window between 3 and 8 PM UTC on Wednesday. Please follow the pinned template in the [#exp-request](https://discord.com/channels/676333549720174605/961491762684235776) channel.

Players may earn a maximum of 3 experience per week; one for writing an awakening or hunting vignette, one for actively working towards an ambition or desire, and one for completing a scene with another character (either another player character or a Storyteller scene). You must post links to all three of these things to get experience for them! If you don't make a request, you will not be awarded experience.

You must post desires in your ticket within 24 hours of your first post for the week.

## PvP Tag

We have two roles here, depending on your comfort level with the unknown and unexpected. These are both detailed in our [#role-selection](https://discord.com/channels/676333549720174605/926988136205123654/928458457097568328) channel. You may change this role at any time. You need to have selected one of these two roles before you are able to post anywhere!
