# ❌ Server-Specific Information

## <mark style="color:red;">Experience Requests</mark>

<mark style="color:red;">Experience is given every Wednesday. Players may earn a maximum of 5xp per week.</mark>

<mark style="color:red;">Roleplay experience is given automatically, but if you wish to get credit for your desire/ambition and awakening/hunting scenes, you must place a request in #exp-request using the template provided in the channel pin.</mark>

#### <mark style="color:red;">Breakdown</mark>

* <mark style="color:red;">1-3xp for active RP (1 granted once each day you post)</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">1xp for an awakening or hunting vignette. You must link the scenes in your XP request for these to count</mark>
* <mark style="color:red;">1xp for meaningfully pursuing your character's desire or ambition</mark>

<mark style="color:red;">Desire must be posted in your ticket within 24 hours of your awakening post in order for it to count!</mark>\
