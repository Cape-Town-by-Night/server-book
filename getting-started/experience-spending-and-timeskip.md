## Time Skip

At the end of every Chronicle (6 in-character nights/3 out-of-character months), the server undergoes a time skip. There will be no roleplay taking place during this time, though you may submit requests for experience spends and write vignettes as desired in the [<mark style="color:red;">Bluebooking</mark>](https://discord.com/channels/1292865933525651466/1359052034900492303) forum.&#x20;

After the time skip date, the in-game date advances by 30 days. The time skip date is treated as a normal reset otherwise. Characters will begin the Chronicle with their Hunger set to 1 and all of their damage, physical and mental, healed.
