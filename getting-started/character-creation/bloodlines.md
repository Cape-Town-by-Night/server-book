<summary>Descendant of Genevieve <em>(Salubri Characters Only)</em> </summary>

Despite her importance, few tales about Genevieve have survived into the modern nights. What is known is that she was a woman well versed in multiple holy scriptures from different cultures. Knowledgeable, kind, and betrayed by alleged allies at least a few

more times than she would have liked, Genevieve was a nun to most, a sister to many, and a teacher to those willing to learn. She was a healer—and still may be—but not in the ways of those like her fellow healer Salubri. She once ran out with a noble’s son to escape the Cainites that found her, and the tale of his safe return is one that is still told to this day.

Being a holy woman, she saw much with her three eyes... perhaps more than she should have. The third eye that a Salubri bears is said to have been a necessary thing, whereby they saw into the other worlds, the lands of the soul and spirit realms. It is known to only appear on the forehead once the most basic practices of their Valeren powers are learned. Perhaps this is why you can trace your heritage back to this woman in particular.

#### • See the Lies in Disguise

Salubri are ever cautious, ever watchful, and always looking over their shoulder. There are those that will betray you at a moment's notice and you’ve been taught to watch for the signs. You can add one bonus die in insight rolls when detecting lies.

#### •• Find the Path

Watchers have a way of knowing things. Guiding those that are lost while oddly knowing more than they should. When faced with a split decision, for one turn, a watcher can call upon a trance-like state to listen to the voices of their guided angels and grant them keener knowledge that might sway the course of a fateful decision in their favour. Once per session, gain two dice to a single Wits or Manipulation roll.

#### ••• Seeing Beyond

The Salubri were always feared for their natural spiritual powers where others had to struggle to see beyond the Shroud. Where death occurred, the watcher will know. Once per story, the whispers of the dead have secrets to share with you. By opening your third eye, you may reduce the difficulty of a single occult roll by 2 (to a minimum of 1).

#### •••• Rebuke the Wicked

Salubri are at constant risk of being diablerized and it is fear from their soul-stealing ‘abilities’ that sets them as targets. Once per session, when threatened, a Salubri can spend 2 willpower to open their third eye and have it glow with blue light, emitting an aura that will keep hostile creatures at bay. If they are within 10 feet, they must make a contested willpower roll to advance. If the attacker loses, the Salubri’s apparently divine light prevents them from advancing closer. This effect lasts for 1 turn per point of margin the Salubri scored on the roll (minimum 1).

#### ••••• Soul Exchange

One of the rarest abilities, the Soul Exchange allows a Salubri to force two individuals to relive each other’s experiences. The guiding vision ends abruptly if either person pulls away but it is indisputably the truth. Costs 1 willpower + 1 willpower per person sharing the vision. The salubri is the one expending the Willpower.

<summary>Descendant of Rayzeel <em>(Salubri Characters Only)</em></summary>

A healer by nature, Rayzeel was known both for her unsurpassed beauty and her unearthly voice. e most cherished childe of Saulot, legend says the Antediluvian Embraced her out of love and is the reason he forbade his childer to ever do the same. Aer the

The slaughter of Samiel during the Baali Wars, it is said that only her singing could rouse him from his sorrow. Her song has been heard throughout the centuries, but what music of hers survives is fragmented. Even those limited remnants, however, show remarkable beauty and complexity of composition.

Rayzeel may have disappeared after her sire fell into torpor, but her legacy lives on. You bear her lineage as a healer of the Salubri Clan. Embraced for your strength, courage, compassion, joy, integrity, and honour, you continue her practices and lead an existence of purity, meditation, and spirituality.

#### • Ashur's Haven

Your need to provide aid is so great, you stay near danger despite the risks to yourself. You aren’t reckless, however, and have taken great pains to keep your haven hidden. Even those that discover it often have trouble remembering much about it or finding their way back to it. They must roll resolve + survival DC 3 to locate it again.

#### •• Sinew and Bone

No one knows healing better than a Salubri. Putting the injured back together takes practice and focus. As such, you may spend a Willpower for Corpore Sano to heal 1 additional level of superficial damage instead of making another Rouse Check.

#### ••• Secrets Lost

The path to enlightenment was something that Rayzeel discovered but could never pass—on or so it is believed. While enlightenment might be out of your reach, the knowledge she discovered sometimes finds its way to you. Once per story, add 3 dice to one medicine, academics or politics roll.

#### •••• Rayzeel's Song

“No Light But Thine”- A song so pure, so beautiful and raw, it soothes the very pain of the heart, soul, and mind. Though most of the composition is lost, you’ve done what you can with your own rendition. This song is enchanting and calming, but simply hearing it can be addictive. Gain the Medicine specialty, First Aid, or Triage. Once per story, you may roll Resolve + Performance, DC 3. Every two successes grant an extra die to Obeah rolls for one scene. When sung, listeners with Humanity 4 or lower may roll Composure + Resolve, DC 4, to gain 1 temporary Humanity for the session. Failure on this roll results in becoming addicted to hearing the song. Addicted individuals treat the Salubri’s bane severity as 1 higher than normal until they eventually pass this roll.

#### ••••• Soul's Inward Gaze

Contemplation and self-reflection are powerful methods of reaching Golconda. But not always is it easy to maintain that balance when you’re helping others maintain their own sanity. Meditation has always been part of a Salubri’s means to purify one’s soul. Once per story, any stains you incurred through helping another can be wiped away with a scene of mediation at the cost of 2 points of Willpower.

</details>
